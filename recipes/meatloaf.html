Meatloaf

Back to Home

Description

Meatloaf is a classic comfort food that is easy to make and delicious. This recipe serves 4-6 people.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large bowl, combine ground beef, onion, and garlic.
  3. Mix well and form into a loaf.
  4. Bake for 45-50 minutes or until internal temperature reaches 160°F (71°C).
  5. Let stand for 5 minutes before serving.
