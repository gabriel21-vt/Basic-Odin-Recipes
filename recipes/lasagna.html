Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish that is perfect for feeding a crowd. This recipe serves 6-8 people.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large skillet, cook ground beef, onion, and garlic over medium heat until meat is no longer pink; drain.
  3. Stir in tomato sauce and seasonings. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. In a greased 9x13 inch baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheese. Repeat layers.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes or until cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Let stand for 10 minutes before serving.
